# v0.9.2 — Living World Foundation & Visual Cohesion

Status: complete.

## Visual-world audit

Before this milestone, Aoi's Shop used a dark interior card with a separately injected portrait; Sakura's Room used a wide illustrated background with coordinate hotspots; Sakura Garden used a full-bleed image, emoji growth layers, and caption overlays; Home used a graphic navy hero with Sakura; Journey was a card-and-rail interface; and the companion page used large isolated portrait presentation. They share the pink/navy palette, rounded surfaces, warm tone, and illustrated character language, but disagreed in where backgrounds were declared, crop behavior, atmosphere, depth boundaries, character scale, and mobile composition. Room and Garden had stronger location identity than Home/Journey, while Shop had the strongest character/environment pairing. Transitions remain intentionally navigation-level rather than cinematic so learning interactions stay immediate.

## Architecture delivered

`src/scenes.js` is the canonical registry and presentation boundary. It registers Aoi's Shop, Sakura's Room, and Sakura Garden with approved WebP background paths, focal and text-safe regions, three responsive framing records, ambient treatment, character-slot guidance, accessible description, and fallback. It also supplies registry validation, lookup/framing helpers, a reusable scene/character renderer for future views, and a DOM mount adapter for the current application architecture.

The mount adapter adds scene identity, responsive crop variables, an inert atmosphere layer, and accessible fallback metadata after each view render. It does not own navigation, progress, placement, discovery, or character reactions. Unknown scenes have a lightweight HTML-safe fallback.

## Compatibility results

- Sakura's Room remains functionally equivalent: its seven `ROOM_ZONES`, fixed ownership restrictions, persistent save-schema placements, hotspot keyboard buttons, and equivalent select/remove controls are unchanged. Cultural sprites remain independent from the room background.
- Aoi's approved environment and expression mapping remain untouched. The Shop opts into shared identity/framing/atmosphere without merging Aoi into Sakura's domain.
- Sakura Garden now has canonical crop, safe character slot, foreground capability, and `growthCompatible` metadata. Existing derived growth score, levels, unlocks, emoji layers, and save semantics remain in `engine.js`.
- The character renderer accepts identity, state, image, anchor, scale, and description. It is deliberately presentation-only and does not unify Sakura/Aoi state machines.
- Ambient treatments are CSS-first, pointer-inert, and nonessential. Motion is disabled by the app setting and OS reduced-motion preference; v0.9.2 adds no constant animation.

## Responsive, accessibility, and performance

Scene metadata specifies intentional desktop, tablet, and approximately 390px mobile crops. Existing Room mobile controls remain one-column and reachable. Environment descriptions remain meaningful; scene art is nonessential; all essential information stays in HTML. Runtime backgrounds remain WebP and are attached only after their view exists, so the registry does not preload other environments. No new image payload was added.

## Art asset handoff

2. Three transparent WebP decorative layers derived from that master: petals (sparse), foliage edge, and lantern/light accent. No text or progress symbols.

2. Home environment/hero background at 2560×1440 with left-side text-safe region and right-side Sakura slot; mobile crop must preserve the face and primary CTA clearance.

All production artwork requires user approval before registration. Random references and generated substitutes are not production assets.

## Validation gate

Focused registry, asset-path, fallback, responsive metadata, Room integration, Shop compatibility, and Garden ownership tests are included in `tests/scenes.test.js`. The full suite passes (43/43), the culture report remains 16 items with 15 illustrated and one intentional text-only entry, the production build succeeds, syntax and diff checks pass, and rendered desktop/tablet inspection confirms Room, Garden, and Shop composition with no console errors. The 390px contract is enforced through explicit mobile framing metadata and existing mobile layout rules; the preview host does not expose device-width emulation, so this breakpoint is covered structurally rather than claimed as a native-device screenshot.
