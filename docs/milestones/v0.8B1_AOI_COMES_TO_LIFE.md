# Nihongo Quest v0.8B.1 — Aoi Comes to Life

## Files and asset mapping

Approved PNG source assets live in `assets/shop/`; visually verified WebP derivatives are copied to `dist/assets/shop/` and used at runtime. The circular shop sign is registered as first-class `SHOP_BRANDING`, alongside the welcoming, smiling, and open-eyed Aoi portraits. The 15 Cultural Collection PNG originals remain the source of truth, with optimized WebP runtime derivatives.

`STOREKEEPER.imageSlots` maps neutral, thoughtful, proud, and surprised to the open-eyed portrait; welcoming to waving; and smiling/amused to the warm closed-eye portrait. `STOREKEEPER_REACTION_MAP` translates restrained companion reaction states into those visual expressions. Dialogue remains in the existing grouped registry and is not duplicated in Shop markup.

## Presentation and behavior

The Shop presentation keeps the v0.8A hierarchy—identity, Aoi/context, featured discovery, collection, and detail—while replacing the placeholder portrait with the mapped Aoi artwork and adding the approved sign as branding. Cultural discovery, knowledge cards, acquisition, audio, and persistence remain on their existing domain paths.

Desktop uses a horizontal Aoi/branding presentation; at narrow widths the portrait and branding stack before the collection grid. Cards remain two columns on small screens, while detail content stays single-column. Aoi’s expression is contextual rather than a reward mechanic; essential meaning is always present as text.

Images include meaningful alt text and safe fallbacks. The sign is labelled as Shop branding, the portrait names Aoi and its semantic expression, and missing images collapse to the existing placeholder. CSS respects the existing reduced-motion class; no new motion is required for the static artwork.

## Verification and remaining gaps

The focused Culture tests verify stable asset paths, state mappings, expression reuse, and dialogue availability. `npm run culture:report`, `npm test`, and `npm run build` are the validation gates. Follow-up integration work makes first and return visits reachable at the Shop-entry boundary, prevents undiscovered cards from requesting artwork, and frames the approved portraits to suppress questionable peripheral background lettering without changing Aoi's source design. `konbini-bag` is deliberately retained as a text-only vocabulary entry until approved art exists. A dedicated layered Shop interior and additional expressions remain deferred unless the corrected Shop still proves incomplete.

## Runtime image optimization

The approved PNG files remain untouched as source assets. Runtime WebP derivatives use quality 90 with exact alpha preservation. Across the 15 cultural objects, four Aoi Shop images, and Sakura's room, runtime weight falls from 40,286,531 bytes to 6,198,748 bytes (84.6%). Per-image alpha channels matched exactly; measured colour RMSE ranged from 2.13 to 5.90 on an 8-bit channel scale. Side-by-side review of the two highest-error object images, Aoi's open portrait, and the room found no material loss of transparency, linework, colour, or fine detail.
