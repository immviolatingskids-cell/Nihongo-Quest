# Sakura Garden v0.9.3 Art Asset Handoff

## Purpose

The approved layered Garden artwork is now integrated for the v0.9.3 environment pass. The seven PNGs remain the source of truth; WebP derivatives are used at runtime.

## Required production assets

1. Garden master: a lossless or otherwise approved source at 2560×1600 or larger. Preserve a central focal region, readable lower-left caption space, and an unobstructed lower-right Sakura slot.
2. Foliage-edge foreground: transparent WebP derived from the master, aligned pixel-for-pixel, text-free, and sparse enough not to cover controls.
3. Petal atmosphere: transparent WebP or CSS-compatible layer with sparse drifting petals, no text, no progress symbols, and no high-frequency visual noise.
4. Lantern/light atmosphere: transparent WebP or CSS-compatible layer with a restrained warm accent, aligned to the master and safe for reduced-motion suppression.
5. Optional growth overlays: early, growing, and full-bloom layers or masks, aligned pixel-for-pixel. These are visual projections only; `gardenProgress`, thresholds, unlocks, milestones, and saves remain canonical in `src/engine.js`.

Transparency is preserved in the runtime derivatives. Only the active growth overlay is mounted for a Garden view.

The supplied files are 1584–1586 × 992–993 px, so they are below the earlier 2560×1600 master recommendation. They are integrated without alteration as approved source material, but a true v0.9.3 stamp still needs either a high-resolution replacement of the same approved artwork or an explicit resolution waiver.

## Crop proofs

Provide proofs at 1440×900, 768×1024, and approximately 390×844. Each proof must preserve the focal subject, lower-left HTML caption/progress area, and lower-right character-safe region. Do not bake captions, progress, labels, buttons, or unlock symbols into any artwork.

## Integration contract

Register approved runtime WebP derivatives in `src/scenes.js` as the Garden master, active growth overlay, foreground, or ambient sources. The renderer loads a layer only when the Garden view exists. Layers are decorative, pointer-inert, hidden from assistive technology, and suppressed or paused under reduced motion. If a layer is unavailable, the scene remains usable with the master background.

## Approval gate

The supplied seven-image set is approved and registered. Crop proofs remain a visual QA responsibility for the browser environment.
